What2Eat FAQ

Answers to common questions about the What2Eat app, its features, PRO plan and your account.

About the app

What is What2Eat?

What2Eat is a free Android app that helps you decide what to cook. It suggests meals from a library of 4,750+ dishes, finds recipes for ingredients you already have, plans menus for guests and builds 7-day meal plans.

Who makes What2Eat?

What2Eat is developed by MFApp, an independent app developer based in Pakistan. You can reach the team at info.mfsoft@gmail.com.

Is What2Eat available on iPhone?

No. What2Eat is currently available for Android phones on Google Play only.

Is What2Eat free?

Yes, What2Eat is free to download and its main features work on the free plan. An optional PRO subscription removes daily and monthly limits and unlocks the Weekly Planner. Prices are shown in the app and on Google Play.

Meals and recipes

How many dishes does What2Eat have?

The library has more than 4,750 dishes. Each includes ingredients, numbered steps, prep time, servings, difficulty and a chef's tip.

Which cuisines are included?

Pakistani home cooking is the largest focus, alongside Indian, Bangladeshi, Afghan, Turkish, Emirati, Saudi, Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Italian, British, American and Mexican dishes.

Which meals can it suggest?

Breakfast, lunch, evening snacks and dinner, with optional sweet dishes and desserts. For Ramadan and Eid, the Special Occasion feature suggests festive and Iftari menus.

Can I read recipes in Urdu or another language?

Yes. Recipes can be shown in English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish or Bengali.

Will it suggest the same dish again and again?

No. When you mark a dish as “Cooking this today”, What2Eat remembers it and avoids suggesting it again for 30 days.

Features

How does the Pantry Matcher work?

Add the ingredients you have, such as eggs, rice, tomatoes or chicken, then tap Find Recipes. What2Eat shows dishes you can make with them. Free users get a limited number of pantry searches per month; PRO users get unlimited searches.

Can What2Eat plan a menu for guests?

Yes. The Guest Feast Planner lets you choose one to seven main dishes plus sweet dishes, generates a balanced menu, and lets you replace individual dishes.

Does What2Eat make weekly meal plans?

Yes. The Weekly Planner creates a 7-day lunch and dinner schedule based on your preferences. It is part of What2Eat PRO.

Can I stop certain dishes from being suggested?

Yes. In Kitchen Preferences you can list dishes you want to cook and dishes you don't want suggested, along with your daily habits.

Can I add my own recipe?

Yes. You can add a community dish with its ingredients, cooking steps, prep time and an optional chef's tip.
