Easy lunch ideas for every day

Homestyle desi lunches, packable boxes for office and school, and fast options when you only have 15 minutes.

By the What2Eat team · Published 17 November 2026 · 7 min read

In this article
  1. Homestyle lunches
  2. Lunches that pack well
  3. Lunch in 15 minutes
  4. Keep lunch varied without extra work
  5. Lunchbox ideas for children

Lunch is the meal most people repeat on autopilot. The same sandwich, the same daal, the same takeaway. A little variety goes a long way, and it doesn't have to take more time.

Homestyle lunches

In many Pakistani and South Asian homes, lunch is a simpler version of dinner: a daal or sabzi with roti or rice.

  • Tarka daal chawal with kachumber salad
  • Aloo baingan or aloo palak with roti
  • Chana daal fry with plain rice
  • Kadhi chawal
  • Mix vegetable sabzi with dahi and roti
  • Lobia (black-eyed beans) curry
  • Leftover salan with fresh roti

Rotate the lentil or vegetable each day. Moong, masoor, chana and mash daal each taste different enough to keep the week interesting.

Lunches that pack well

For office or school, choose food that tastes good at room temperature or reheats well.

  • Paratha rolls: chicken, egg or aloo filling rolled in a paratha and wrapped in foil.
  • Pulao or fried rice: holds up well and reheats easily.
  • Pasta salad: pasta with vegetables, chickpeas and a lemon or yoghurt dressing.
  • Chicken sandwiches: use leftover chicken with mayonnaise, salad and a little black pepper.
  • Chana chaat: filling, cheap and needs no reheating.
  • Shami kabab with bun: a homemade burger that travels well.

Lunch in 15 minutes

  • Egg fried rice from leftover rice
  • Instant noodles upgraded with vegetables and an egg
  • Cheese and vegetable quesadilla using a tortilla or roti
  • Tuna or chickpea salad wrap
  • Masoor daal (red lentils cook in about 15 minutes)

Keep lunch varied without extra work

  1. Cook extra at dinner. Tonight's karahi is tomorrow's paratha roll.
  2. Change one element. Same daal, different side: rice one day, roti the next, a salad on the third.
  3. Try one new cuisine a week. A Chinese-style stir-fry or Italian pasta salad uses ingredients you already buy.
  4. Prep components, not meals. Boiled chickpeas, cooked rice and chopped salad can be combined differently every day.

Lunchbox ideas for children

Children tend to like small portions and familiar flavours. Mini paratha rolls, pasta, chicken nuggets made at home, fruit, and a small sweet like a homemade cookie usually come back empty. Keep spice levels mild and include one food you know they'll eat.

Quick answers

What's a healthy, easy lunch?

Daal with rice and salad, or a wholewheat wrap with chicken or chickpeas and vegetables. Both are balanced and quick.

What lunch can I make the night before?

Pasta salad, pulao, chana chaat and paratha rolls all keep well overnight in the fridge.
