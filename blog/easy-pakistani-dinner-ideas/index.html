Easy Pakistani dinner ideas for every night of the week

Weeknight daal and sabzi, faster karahi, and weekend pulao: Pakistani dinners organised by time and occasion.

By the What2Eat team · Published 17 November 2026 · 8 min read

  2. Everyday homestyle dinners
  3. Weekend and special-day dinners

Pakistani home cooking has a clear rhythm. Weeknights are for daal, sabzi and simple salan with roti. Weekends and guest nights bring out karahi, pulao, biryani and nihari. The trick to easy dinners is knowing which dishes are quick, which can be made ahead, and which are worth saving for a special day.

Dinners in about 30 minutes

  • Masoor daal with roti or rice. Red lentils cook in 15 to 20 minutes without soaking. Finish with a tarka of garlic, cumin and red chilli in hot oil or ghee.
  • Chicken karahi. Boneless or small-cut chicken cooked hard with tomatoes, ginger, garlic and green chillies. No onion base needed, which saves time.
  • Aloo anday. Potato and boiled egg curry, a classic budget dinner.
  • Keema matar. Mince and peas cook quickly and go with roti or rice.
  • Bhindi masala or aloo gobi. Dry vegetable dishes that are ready in the time it takes to make roti.
  • Egg bhurji with paratha. When nothing else is ready.

Everyday homestyle dinners

These are the dishes most Pakistani families eat several times a month. They're not fancy, but they're comforting and balanced.

  • Daal chawal with kachumber salad and achar
  • Kadhi pakora with plain rice
  • Aloo gosht shorba with roti
  • Chana daal with lauki or palak
  • Mix vegetable sabzi with dahi
  • Chicken salan with roti
  • Moong masoor daal with palak

Weekend and special-day dinners

When you have more time or company, these are worth the effort.

How to make desi cooking faster

  1. Keep ginger-garlic paste ready. Blend a jar once a week and refrigerate. It saves several minutes every day.
  2. Fry onions in bulk. Browned onions keep for days in the fridge and form the base of salan, pulao and biryani.
  3. Use a pressure cooker for lentils, chickpeas and tougher cuts of meat.
  4. Freeze roti dough or parathas. Half-cooked parathas reheat well on a tawa.
  5. Cook one rich dish and one light dish. A karahi with a simple daal stretches further than two heavy curries.

Sides that complete the meal

A Pakistani dinner feels complete with a few small extras: raita (yoghurt with cucumber, mint or boondi), kachumber salad, sliced onions with lemon, achar, and green chutney. They take minutes and make a simple daal feel like a full meal.

Quick answers

What is the easiest Pakistani dinner for beginners?

Masoor daal with rice. It uses few ingredients, cooks quickly and is hard to get wrong.

What can I cook for dinner in Pakistan when I'm short on time?

Chicken karahi, keema matar, aloo anday or egg bhurji can all be on the table in about half an hour.

Which Pakistani dishes can be made ahead?

Nihari, haleem, shami kababs, yakhni for pulao and most salans taste as good or better the next day.
