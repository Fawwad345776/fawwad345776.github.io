What2Eat features

A meal decider, a pantry matcher, a guest planner and a weekly planner in one Android app, all working from a library of 4,750+ dishes.

Free onGoogle Play

Meal Decider

The Meal Decider is the heart of What2Eat. It answers "what should I cook today?" with a specific dish, not a list of 50 options.

  • Meal time: breakfast, lunch, evening snacks or dinner. The library also includes Iftari dishes for Ramadan.
  • Number of main dishes: one to five, for a simple dinner or a fuller spread.
  • Cooking occasion: a normal day means everyday homestyle food like daal roti, sabzi roti, daal chawal and kadhi chawal. A special day means feasts, fast food and BBQ: biryani, pulao, nihari, paye, burgers and pizza.
  • Sweet dishes: switch on to add desserts such as kheer, halwa, gulab jamun or custard.

Each suggestion shows the cooking time, cuisine and difficulty. Tap the dish for the full recipe, swap a single dish you don't feel like, or ask for more suggestions. A 30-day anti-repetition memory keeps recently cooked dishes out of the way.

Pantry Matcher

Pantry Matcher works the other way round: you start with ingredients, and What2Eat finds what you can cook with them.

  • Type any ingredient (eggs, rice, paneer, chicken) or use quick-add chips for common staples like tomatoes, onions, garlic and ginger.
  • Tap Find Recipes to see dishes that match what you have.
  • Free users get a set number of pantry searches each month. PRO makes them unlimited.

It's the fastest way to use up vegetables before they spoil. Our guide on cooking with ingredients you already have shows how to get the most from it.

Guest Feast Planner

Hosting is where deciding gets hard. The Guest Feast Planner builds a balanced dawat menu so you can focus on cooking.

  • Choose one to seven main dishes and how many sweet dishes you want.
  • Generate a feast menu, then replace any dish that doesn't fit your guests.
  • Open each dish for ingredients and steps, and save the menu to your meal plan.

Weekly Planner

The Weekly Planner lays out lunch and dinner for all seven days. Set your preferences, let AI auto-suggest the week, and refresh any day you want to change.

  • A full 7-day schedule in one screen, so you can shop once.
  • Routine days and special days, matched to your preferences.
  • Available with What2Eat PRO.

New to planning ahead? Start with how meal planning makes cooking easier.

Special Occasion menus

Ramadan, Eid and dawat each have their own food traditions. The Special Occasion feature generates traditional and festive menus, from Iftari ideas to an Eid lunch spread, and lets you save them to your weekly plan.

Dine Out

Some evenings nobody wants to cook. Dine Out gives you a daily dining suggestion and a list of nearby restaurants for your chosen city.

  • Sort by top rated or nearest.
  • See signature dishes, distance and whether a place is open.
  • Save favourites, hide places you don't like, and get directions in one tap.

Kitchen Preferences

Every household is different. Kitchen Preferences lets you tell What2Eat which dishes you want to cook, which you never want suggested and your daily food habits. Suggestions across the app follow these settings.

Full recipes in 7 languages

Dishes come with ingredients, numbered steps, prep time, servings and a chef's tip. With AI-powered translation you can read a recipe in English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish or Bengali, which helps when the person cooking prefers a different language from the person planning.

Community dishes

Your family's recipe might be someone else's next dinner. Add a dish with its ingredients, steps, prep time and an optional chef's tip, and browse and like dishes shared by other What2Eat cooks.

Cooked history

When you tap "Cooking this today", the dish is added to your cooked history. What2Eat uses it for the 30-day anti-repetition memory, and you can look back at what you made.
