About What2Eat

What2Eat is an Android app that helps households decide what to cook, every day. It is built by MFApp, an independent app developer based in Pakistan.

Why we built it

In many homes the hardest part of cooking happens before anyone picks up a knife. Someone has to decide. Lunch was daal yesterday, the kids don't want sabzi, there's chicken in the freezer but no plan, and it's already 6 pm. That small decision, made two or three times a day, every day, is tiring.

What2Eat was built to take that decision off your plate. Instead of scrolling through recipe sites, you tell the app the meal time and the kind of day, and it gives you a specific answer you can cook.

What the app does

  • Meal Decider suggests breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, dinner and sweets.
  • Pantry Matcher finds recipes for ingredients you already have.
  • Guest Feast Planner builds balanced menus for dawats and dinner parties.
  • Weekly Planner creates a 7-day lunch and dinner plan.
  • Special Occasion menus cover Ramadan, Eid and other celebrations.
  • Dine Out suggests nearby restaurants when you'd rather not cook.

The dish library

The app works from a library of more than 4,750 dishes. Each one has ingredients, numbered steps, prep time, servings, difficulty and a chef's tip. The library began with Pakistani home cooking and now includes Indian, Bangladeshi, Afghan, Turkish, Emirati, Saudi, Chinese, Thai, Japanese, Italian, British, American and Mexican dishes. Dishes are tagged as everyday "normal day" food or "special day" food, so a Tuesday suggestion looks different from an Eid one.

How AI is used

What2Eat uses AI to help with meal suggestions, weekly plan auto-suggest and recipe translation into seven languages. The dish library itself is curated, so suggestions come from real recipes with real steps.
